How it works with TikTok

The tool uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (Direct Post) on the DataFiesta TikTok account only.

1. Connect the account

The creator clicks "Connect TikTok" and signs in on TikTok's own authorization page. The tool requests these scopes:

  • user.info.basic: show the connected nickname and avatar, so the creator knows which account will receive the post;
  • video.publish: post the clip the creator has approved;
  • user.info.stats and video.list: read follower count and the statistics of the account's own videos.

2. Prepare the post

  1. The creator opens the "Post to TikTok" window for a clip of the publishing plan and watches a preview of the video.
  2. The window shows the TikTok nickname the video will be posted to, and the caption, which the creator can edit.
  3. The privacy level is chosen by the creator from the options returned by TikTok for this account (no default is pre-selected).
  4. Comments, Duet and Stitch can be allowed or turned off; options disabled on the account are shown as unavailable.
  5. A "Disclose video content" switch lets the creator mark promotional content ("Your brand" or "Branded content"), with the matching TikTok policy notice.
  6. By posting, the creator agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation (and Branded Content Policy when disclosed).

3. Post and follow up

After the creator clicks "Post", the video file is sent to TikTok. The tool checks the publishing status and tells the creator that processing may take a few minutes before the video appears on the profile. Statistics of the post are then read at day 1, 3, 7 and 28.

What the tool never does

  • It never posts without an explicit click from the creator on that clip.
  • It never accesses any other TikTok account or any other user's data.
  • It never sells, shares or uses data for advertising (see the Privacy Policy).

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok: Settings › Security › Manage app permissions.
