Frequently asked questions

What is DataFiesta?

A French YouTube channel that tells stories with data (music, lottery, society). Each long video is cut into short clips for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram.

Who can use DataFiesta Publication?

The tool is operated by the DataFiesta creator to manage the channel's own accounts. It is not offered to other creators and has no sign-up.

Why does the app run at 127.0.0.1?

DataFiesta Publication is a desktop app: it runs on the creator's own computer and is used in the browser at a local address. Video files never leave that computer except to be posted to the connected accounts.

Which TikTok data does it use?

The connected account's basic profile (nickname, avatar), follower count, and the list and statistics of the account's own videos. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Where is the data stored?

On the creator's computer and in a private Google spreadsheet. It is never sold or shared.

How do I remove the tool's access?

TikTok: Settings › Security › Manage app permissions. Google: myaccount.google.com/connections. Instagram and LinkedIn: in their app settings.

How do I contact you?

By email: get.datafiesta@gmail.com.
