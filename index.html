Publish every DataFiesta video, everywhere, on time.

DataFiesta Publication is the in-house tool of DataFiesta, a French data-storytelling channel. It plans, publishes and tracks the channel's own videos on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn from one calendar.

See the featuresHow TikTok posting works

What it does

One publishing calendar

Each DataFiesta video becomes a long version and a series of short clips. The tool lays them out over several weeks, one post a day, per network.

Direct posting to TikTok

The creator reviews each clip, picks its privacy level and interaction settings, then posts it to the DataFiesta TikTok account with the Content Posting API.

Results in one place

Views, likes, comments and shares of each post are collected at fixed milestones (day 1, 3, 7, 28) to compare networks and formats.

Comments inbox

New comments from YouTube, Instagram and LinkedIn are gathered in one list so the creator can answer the community faster.

Who uses it

DataFiesta Publication is built and used by the creator of the DataFiesta channel to manage the channel's official accounts. It only ever publishes content owned by DataFiesta, on accounts that the creator has connected through each platform's official login.

It is a desktop app: it runs on the creator's computer and opens in the browser at a local address (127.0.0.1). There is no online account or login page; this website presents the app and its policies.
