Features

Everything the DataFiesta channel needs between "the video is ready" and "the video is live everywhere".

Screenshots

The app interface is in French, the creator's language.

Planning

Publishing plan per video

One line per clip and per network: title, description, hashtags, date and time. Texts are written for each network.

Calendar and reminders

A week-by-week calendar shows what goes out when. Reminders tell the creator when a post needs a manual step.

Validation before sending

Nothing is published until the creator has approved the whole plan. A single invalid line blocks the batch.

Publishing

TikTok

Direct Post with the Content Posting API: the creator's TikTok nickname is shown, privacy level is chosen from the options TikTok allows for the account, comments, Duet and Stitch can be turned off, and commercial content can be disclosed.

YouTube

Upload and scheduling of long videos and Shorts, playlists, "made for kids" and AI-content declarations.

Instagram and LinkedIn

Reels, image carousels and posts on the DataFiesta Instagram account and LinkedIn page.

Analytics

Milestone statistics

Each post is measured at day 1, 3, 7 and 28 so that every network is compared on the same basis.

Network comparison

The same clip published on several networks is compared side by side: reach, reactions, followers gained.

Follower tracking

A daily snapshot of followers and total views for each account.
